# **SAE Week of Welcome Recruitment**

I helped represent CSULB SAE during Week of Welcome to increase awareness of Formula SAE, Baja SAE, and other opportunities within the organization.

Over two days, we spoke with students about the club's engineering projects, technical subteams, manufacturing opportunities, and ways to become involved.

## **Results**
 - Generated 300+ new-member sign-ups over two days
 - Introduced prospective members to Formula SAE and Baja SAE technical opportunities
 - Helped communicate engineering concepts and team responsibilities to students with varying levels of technical experience
 - Supported recruitment efforts aimed at expanding the club's active membership

Once membership dues were finalized, 236 students converted to paid members
