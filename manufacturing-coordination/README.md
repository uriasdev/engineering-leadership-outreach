# Manufacturing Coordination

Through organizing the welding workshops, I identified and highlighted the need for more structured manufacturing support within SAE. Coordinating equipment access, technician availability, safety requirements, and member training made it clear that Formula SAE and Baja SAE would benefit from a more organized approach to shared shop resources.

These discussions helped support the development of a dedicated manufacturing team intended to coordinate shop usage, fabrication activities, and manufacturing resources across both Formula SAE and Baja SAE.

This experience showed me how technical events can reveal broader organizational needs and how engineering leadership can extend beyond design work into process improvement, resource coordination, and team development.
